# what if no. 04 · What if you jumped into a tunnel through the Earth?

50 s colourful 2D motion-graphics short (1080×1920, 30 fps), same editorial typography system as no. 03 but a
multi-colour palette (cobalt, tomato, mint, pink, sunflower, tangerine). Risograph-style illustrations made with
Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5; background removal; three 5 s Kling 3.0 card loops), animated as paper cutouts in
`src/scene.html` (rendered with `src/render.mjs`). Earth layers, globe and counters are drawn for this video.

Beats: title → Sydney card + globe (12,742 km) → 3·2·1 JUMP → descent with TIME / DEPTH / SPEED counters through
crust, mantle, outer core, inner core → the centre (19 min, ≈36,000 km/h, weightless) → back up → Atlantic splash
(≈38 min, ≈480 km SW of the Azores) → Kola Superdeep Borehole 12,262 m (≈0.2%) with the bucket-hat guy → end card.

Facts: counters use `src/table.json`, computed from the PREM density model (reproduces Klotz 2015: 38 min 11 s trip).
Layers: USGS. Core temperature: Anzellini et al. 2013 (≈6,000 °C at the inner-core boundary) / NatGeo ≈5,200 °C.
Antipode of Sydney 33.87° N, 28.79° W (computed). Kola: EarthDate (UT Austin). Air drag / spin: Physics Today 2016,
UAF Geophysical Institute.

Rights: AI-generated illustrations (Higgsfield), labelled in captions; fonts Inter Display, IBM Plex Mono (OFL);
SFX + music ElevenLabs (flow "lost_index · what if 04 · tunnel through earth").
