This pink paint
supposedly ran out
because of one movie.
In twenty twenty-two,
designers built Barbieland
in England for Greta Gerwig.
She wanted the pinks
very bright,
and everything almost too much.
Designer Sarah Greenwood
needed a fluorescent pink
from Rosco,
a scenic paint supplier.
Greenwood says they asked
for two hundred liters
of pure pigment.
Rosco, already short
after pandemic delays,
told the Los Angeles Times
the production cleaned them out.
Greenwood told Architectural Digest
the world ran out of pink.
Headlines ran with it.
Later, she called it
an offhand remark.
Pink paint turned up
in backyard workshops.
The shortage was real.
It just belonged to one company,
not the planet.
Too much pink,
or just enough?
