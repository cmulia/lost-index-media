# Explained no. 14: James Cameron sold The Terminator for one dollar

Opening style B (question with stakes as first spoken line). Scheduled Mon 16 Nov 2026 (Sydney).

## Narration

Would you sell your best idea for one dollar? James Cameron did. He says it began in nineteen eighty-one, with a fever in a cheap Rome hotel. He dreamed of a chrome skeleton climbing out of a fire. He sketched it on hotel stationery, then wrote the script. As he tells it, he sold it to producer Gale Anne Hurd for one dollar, on one condition: he would direct. The budget was about six point four million dollars, and the shoot was rushed. The Terminator reached cinemas in nineteen eighty-four. A franchise followed. Years later, Cameron said he wished he had not sold the rights. But the dollar bought him the one thing he wanted: the chair. Would you have sold it too, or kept the rights?
