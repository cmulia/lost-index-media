She beat her ex-husband's blockbuster for Best Director. March seventh, twenty ten. Kathryn Bigelow had made The Hurt Locker, a war film about a bomb disposal team in Iraq. It cost about fifteen million dollars. Avatar, from her former husband James Cameron, had already earned over two billion. Barbra Streisand opened the envelope. "Well, the time has come," she said. Bigelow became the first woman ever to win Best Director. Her film also won Best Picture, though it had made only around fifteen million dollars at the American box office. Avatar took three awards: art direction, cinematography and visual effects. Cameron was sitting right behind her. He later told the New York Times he was "the first one on my feet applauding." The man she beat, by his own account, stood up first.
