Who bets their own house on a movie? Francis Ford Coppola did. In nineteen seventy-six, he took his crew to the Philippines to shoot Apocalypse Now. It was meant to cost twelve million dollars. A typhoon hit, and the shoot ran over a year. Newsweek reported he had put up all his own assets as guarantees on the loans. The film won the top prize at Cannes. The bet paid off. Decades later, he did it again. Megalopolis reportedly cost one hundred twenty million dollars, and he funded it himself. He sold part of his wine business to raise it. Here is the quiet part. That wine business began with Godfather profits. A film paid for the vines, and the vines paid for a film. Bold gamble or reckless folly?
