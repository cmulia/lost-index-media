Why did Disney let Tim Burton go over a dog? In nineteen eighty-four, Burton made a black-and-white short called Frankenweenie, about a boy who revives his dead dog. It got a PG rating. That reportedly ended Disney's plan to pair it with Pinocchio. Burton says the ratings board told him: "There's nothing you can cut. It's just the tone." Disney shelved the short, and Burton left, though he calls it a parting of ways. Paul Reubens had seen the short. Burton soon directed Pee-wee's Big Adventure. In twenty twelve, Disney released his feature-length stop-motion remake. Still black and white. Still PG. The film too dark for Disney became a Disney film. Too dark for kids, or just right?
