Narration (132 words): "His lost films may have become boot heels. Georges Méliès was among the first to film made-up stories. In nineteen oh two, his rocket landed in the Moon's eye. Then came the First World War. The story goes that in nineteen seventeen, the army melted hundreds of his reels, the celluloid for boot heels. By nineteen twenty-three, the Pathé company had taken his property, and he destroyed what was left. He ended up in a small toy and sweets stand at Montparnasse station. In nineteen twenty-nine, a man named Mauclaire was given old reels found in a Normandy dairy shed. He tracked Méliès down, and Paris threw him a gala. More than two hundred of his films survive today. His family made luxury boots. In the end, the films outlasted the boots."
