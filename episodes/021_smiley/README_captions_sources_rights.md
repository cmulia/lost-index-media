## Instagram caption
The smiley was invented because a joke backfired. 🙂

In September 1982, a Carnegie Mellon online bulletin board lit up over a warning that an elevator was contaminated with mercury. It was a joke, and some readers missed that.

On 19 September, computer scientist Scott Fahlman proposed marking jokes with a colon, a hyphen and a bracket, to be read sideways: :-) . He also suggested :-( for posts that were not jokes.

The original post was later lost. In 2002, CMU staff recovered it from old backup tapes (retrieved 10 September 2002).
