Satyajit Ray pawned
his wife's jewellery
to keep filming.
In nineteen fifty-two
he began his first film,
Pather Panchali.
He had already borrowed
against his insurance policy.
By nineteen fifty-three,
the money had run out.
Shooting stopped
for almost a year.
Then the West Bengal government
agreed to fund the film.
The film was released
in nineteen fifty-five.
In nineteen ninety-two,
the Academy honoured him
for his rare mastery
of the art of motion pictures.
He was too ill to travel.
Audrey Hepburn introduced him,
and a crew filmed his thanks
from a hospital bed
in Calcutta.
Twenty-four days later,
he was gone.
He once risked everything
to begin.
At the end,
Hollywood came to him.
Risk everything for art,
or play safe?
