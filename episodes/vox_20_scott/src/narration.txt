Why did Apple try to sell its most famous ad? In December nineteen eighty-three, Apple's board watched the new Macintosh commercial. Chief executive John Sculley later said most felt it was the worst commercial they had seen. The director was Ridley Scott, fresh from Blade Runner. He filmed it in England, casting local skinheads as the shaven, grey workers. Sculley told the agency, Chiat Day, to sell the airtime. It sold thirty seconds and said it was too late for the rest. Steve Wozniak says he even offered to pay half the airtime himself. So the full ad aired once, during the Super Bowl on January twenty-second, nineteen eighty-four. The ad Apple tried to sell is now the one everyone remembers. Bold gamble, or lucky escape?
