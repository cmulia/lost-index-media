This scratched little film reel holds footage everyone thought was gone. In nineteen twenty-seven, Fritz Lang premiered Metropolis at about two and a half hours. Soon it was trimmed, and the cuts vanished. Even careful restorations were still missing about a quarter of the film. In two thousand eight, a Buenos Aires curator pulled out reels until she found a scene nobody knew. The story goes that a collector carried a print to Argentina in nineteen twenty-eight. More than twenty minutes came back, badly scratched. In twenty ten, a restoration premiered in Berlin, nearly whole again. It was never hidden. The museum had been on a distribution list since the nineteen twenties. Someone just had to ask. Keep scratched footage, or hide the damage?
