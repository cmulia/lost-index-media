## Instagram caption (draft, not posted)

In 2000, Netflix's founders say they flew to Dallas with an offer: Blockbuster could buy their company for $50 million. Blockbuster passed.

Why so easy? Rewind. In 1976 JVC put the first VHS recorder on sale. Sony's Betamax got there first, but early Beta tapes held one hour and VHS held two. VHS won. In October 1985 the first Blockbuster opened in Dallas. By 2004 there were about 9,000.

Then came the DVD: thin, light, cheap to mail. Netflix opened for business in April 1998. At the Dallas meeting, Marc Randolph later wrote, Blockbuster's CEO John Antioco was "struggling not to laugh." Antioco remembers it differently and says there were never serious talks.

Either way, no deal. Netflix started streaming in January 2007. Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy in September 2010. Netflix mailed its last DVD in 2023.

One Blockbuster is still open, in Bend, Oregon. In 2021 a documentary about it found a home: on Netflix.
