## Facts / numbers
- Surface speed from Earth's rotation at Venice (45.4°N): 1,670 km/h × cos 45.4° ≈ 1,170 km/h ("about 1,200 km/h").
- With gravity gone, a body keeps its velocity in a straight line; relative to the ground it rises by ½·ω²R·cos²φ·t²
  (ω = 7.29×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, R = 6,371 km): ≈ 0.85 m after 10 s at Venice (plus ≈ 0.85 m southward). Our own estimate.
- ISS: gravity at ~400 km altitude is ≈ 90% of surface gravity; orbital speed ≈ 28,000 km/h (NASA).
