## Instagram caption (draft, not posted)

In a darkened theatre, a singer walked off stage in the middle of a song. Nobody noticed. Her voice kept singing.

From 1915, Edison's "tone tests" toured America. A singer performed beside a phonograph playing her own recording, the lights went down, and when they came back up only the machine was left. Audiences couldn't tell when she'd gone.

But it was carefully staged. Edison chose singers who could imitate their own records. Soprano Anna Case later said she matched her voice to the machine.

🎧 Sound on: that's Anna Case herself, on a 1915 Edison Diamond Disc.
